# Task Closeout

Keep this concise and include verifiable references.

Post a stage completion record on the existing GitHub task/parent issue and reply
to the requester: task ID/stage, identity, result, exact candidate SHA and author
where applicable, checks/limits, and proposed next responsible participant/action.
Follow [task intake](../docs/governance/task-intake.md) for privacy and offline
pending-sync handling; local evidence supplements the issue. The Manager owns
successor dispatch and acceptance. Intermediate/feature PRs use `Refs #N` only.

For an accepted task, prepare a private [documentation refresh proposal](../docs/operations/documentation-refresh.md)
from exact accepted candidate/source hashes. Reconcile status wording, category/global links and
authorized material-index inventory. Preserve history and owner promotion gates; recheck revisions
before review. A capture receipt or uncommitted Dreamer proposal is not acceptance.

Do not record raw hidden reasoning, secrets, personal data or full conversations. Record outcomes, decisions, evidence, errors and resolutions.
